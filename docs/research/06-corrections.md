# Corrections to the research (found after publication)

Each entry: what the research said, what is correct, the source that was read, and where the site changed. Added in Phase 5 after the reader critic's round 0.

## S1 — the seed and the date stone (6:95)

- **Research said:** "Taking up water triggers gibberellin (GA) signalling. Amylase enzymes turn stored starch into sugar." This is the textbook model for cereal grains, but 6:95 names both grain (*ḥabb*) and the date stone (*nawā*), and the page applied it to the date stone too.
- **Correct:** A date stone's main stored carbohydrate is mannan (galactomannan), laid down in the thick walls of its endosperm cells, which is also what makes it so hard. Endo-β-mannanase, the enzyme that breaks these walls down, rises in the micropylar endosperm only after germination is complete (the root has emerged). The page's animation was reordered to match: the food is broken down after the root appears.
- **Source (read):** Gong X, Bassel GW, Wang A, Greenwood JS, Bewley JD (2005). "The Emergence of Embryos from Hard Seeds is Related to the Structure of the Cell Walls of the Micropylar Endosperm, and not to Endo-β-mannanase Activity." *Annals of Botany* 96(7):1165–1173. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4247068 — date endosperm walls "composed of galactomannan"; enzyme activity rose "after the completion of germination"
- **Site:** `src/content/signs/s01-seed.yaml` (science layer, visualization step, sources), both languages.

## M14, M8 — two weak hadith labelled too kindly (content critic, Phase 5)

- **Research said:** M14, "Seeking knowledge is an obligation on every Muslim" (Ibn Mājah 224; "chain debated, graded *ḥasan* by some through multiple routes"). M8, "The honest, trustworthy merchant is with the prophets…" (Tirmidhī 1209; "graded weak-to-fair by some").
- **Source data:** Ibn Mājah 224 — Very Daif (al-Albānī, M. Fuʾād ʿAbd al-Bāqī, Shuʿayb al-Arnaʾūṭ), Daif (Zubair ʿAlī Zaʾī). Tirmidhī 1209 — Daif (al-Albānī, Aḥmad Shākir), Hasan (Bashshār ʿAwwād Maʿrūf). No grader named in the research could be checked for the "multiple routes" view.
- **Site:** both now carry a grade token, so the page shows the graders' own grades, and both are stated not to be used as evidence (Umut's rule: no claim rests on a weak hadith). Ibn Mājah 425 (M12) also carries a token; it was already labelled weak.

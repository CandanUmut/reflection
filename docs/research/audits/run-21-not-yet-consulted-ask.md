## Q7 — Is leaving Islam punishable by death?
- **Limit:** "The cases at the conquest of Mecca (Ibn Khatal, Ibn Abi Sarh) have not yet been checked."
  **Verdict:** Checked — limit can be removed
  **Source:** Sunan Abi Dawud 2685 (hadithunlocked.com), Anas: "Ibn Akhtal is hanging with the curtains of the Ka'bah." He said "Kill him" (al-Albani: *ṣaḥīḥ*); the hadith gives no reason. *ʿAwn al-Maʿbūd*, citing al-Khattabi, says Ibn Khatal had been sent out with an Ansari companion, killed him on the way and took his property. Sunan Abi Dawud 4359 (hadithunlocked.com), from Saʿd: ʿAbdullah ibn Saʿd ibn Abi Sarh "hid himself with Uthman ibn Affan" at the conquest; ʿUthman brought him and asked for his pledge to be accepted; the Prophet (pbuh) refused twice and accepted the third time, then asked why no one had stood up to kill him, and said a prophet does not signal deceptively with his eyes (al-Albani: *ṣaḥīḥ*; al-Arnaʾut: chain *ḥasan*; al-Mundhiri notes criticism of al-Suddi).
  **New text:** At the conquest of Mecca, the Prophet (pbuh) ordered Ibn Khatal killed even as he clung to the Kaʿba's curtains ({hadith:abudawud:2685}); the hadith gives no reason, and the commentators explain that he had murdered the Muslim companion travelling with him. ʿAbdullah ibn Abi Sarh, who had also left Islam, was spared: ʿUthman interceded, and the Prophet (pbuh), after hesitating, accepted his pledge ({hadith:abudawud:4359}). The two cases are used on both sides of the modern debate: one ends in execution, apparently for murder; the other in pardon.

## Q23 — Polygyny
- **Limit:** "Ibn Kathir and al-Tabari on 4:129 have not yet been consulted, so no classical holder is named"; "a named classical source for it [the 'in feeling' reading] has not yet been consulted here".
  **Verdict:** Checked (Ibn Kathir) — limit can be narrowed to al-Tabari
  **Source:** Ibn Kathir on 4:129 (abridged English, surahquran.com): even with a just division of nights, "there will still be various degrees concerning love, desire and sexual intimacy"; this view is reported from Ibn ʿAbbas, ʿUbayda al-Salmani, Mujahid, al-Hasan al-Basri and al-Dahhak ibn Muzahim; the Prophet (pbuh) divided equally and said "O Allah! This is my division in what I own, so do not blame me for what You own and I do not own" — "the heart" — from Abu Dawud, and Ibn Kathir says "its chain of narrators is Sahih". 4:129 (Saheeh): "And you will never be able to be equal [in feeling] between wives, even if you should strive [to do so]. So do not incline completely [toward one] and leave another hanging."
  **New text:** Ibn Kathir, reporting Ibn ʿAbbas, ʿUbayda al-Salmani, Mujahid, al-Hasan al-Basri and al-Dahhak, explains that even when a husband divides his nights justly, "there will still be various degrees concerning love, desire and sexual intimacy" — that is the equality 4:129 says cannot be reached. What can be controlled — time and maintenance — must be equal, and he must not "incline completely" and leave a wife "hanging" (4:129). The Prophet (pbuh) used to pray: "O Allah! This is my division in what I own, so do not blame me for what You own and I do not own", meaning the heart (reported by Abu Dawud; Ibn Kathir: chain *ṣaḥīḥ*).

- **Limits:** *Tafsir al-Manar*; Mawdudi; the Indonesian study; the four schools' detailed rules; divorce for harm.
  **Verdict:** Not consulted in this pass — limits stay.

## Q13 — Is eternal Hell just for finite sins?
- **Limit:** "the classical commentaries on 11:107… have not yet been checked directly."
  **Verdict:** Checked (Ibn Kathir) — limit can be narrowed to al-Tabari, al-Qurtubi and al-Razi
  **Source:** Ibn Kathir on 11:107 (abridged English, surahquran.com): "It has been said that the exception mentioned in this verse refers to the disobedient among the people of Tawhid"; "It is these whom Allah will bring out of the Fire by the intercession of the interceders"; after the last intercession "No one remains in the Fire… except those who will remain there forever without escape". He cites Ibn Jarir (al-Tabari) only for "as long as the heavens and the earth endure" being an Arab idiom for eternity. 11:107 (Saheeh): "[They will be] abiding therein as long as the heavens and the earth endure, except what your Lord should will. Indeed, your Lord is an effecter of what He intends."
  **New text:** Ibn Kathir reads the exception in 11:107 ("except what your Lord should will") as referring to "the disobedient among the people of Tawhid", believers who sinned and will be brought out of the Fire by intercession; those who remain after that, he says, remain forever. He takes "as long as the heavens and the earth endure" as an Arab way of saying "forever", following al-Tabari.

## Q8 — Intrusive thoughts
- **Limit:** "Awad cites an international study (Radomsky et al., 2014) estimating that 'about 94% of the human population worldwide experience intrusive thoughts'; the study's own sample has not yet been checked."
  **Verdict:** Checked — limit can be removed, and the figure narrowed
  **Source:** Radomsky et al., "Part 1 — You can run but you can't hide: Intrusive thoughts on six continents", *Journal of Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders* 3(3) (2014) 269–279 (University of Nicosia record): "(n=777) university students at 15 sites in 13 countries across 6 continents"; "nearly all participants (93.6%) reported experiencing at least one intrusion during the previous three months".
  **New text:** In a study of 777 university students at 15 sites in 13 countries on six continents, 93.6% reported at least one unwanted intrusive thought in the previous three months (Radomsky et al., 2014). The study was of students, not of "the human population", but the finding is clear: such thoughts are nearly universal.

  **Verdict:** Not consulted in this pass — limits stay.

## Q5 — Evolution
- **Limit:** 3:59 "its occasion of revelation has not yet been checked".
  **Verdict:** Checked — limit can be removed
  **Source:** Ibn Kathir on 3:59 (abridged English, surahquran.com), from Muhammad ibn Ishaq's *Sīra*: "a delegation from the Christians of Najran (in Yemen) came to Al-Madinah to argue about 'Isa", claiming he was divine; Ibn Kathir: "for Allah created Adam without a father or a mother".
  **New text:** The verse came, according to Ibn Ishaq as reported by Ibn Kathir, when a Christian delegation from Najran argued in Medina that Jesus (pbuh) was divine; its point is that Adam, created "without a father or a mother", is the greater wonder. It answers a question about Jesus; it was not given to settle biology.

## Q20 — Aisha's age
- **Limit:** Iraq's 2025 amendment "has not yet been checked".
  **Verdict:** Checked — correction
  **Source:** Human Rights Watch, "Iraq: Personal Status Law Amendment Sets Back Women's Rights" (10 March 2025): passed on 21 January 2025, in force 17 February 2025; couples may choose the 1959 law or a Jaʿfari code (*mudawwana*) and cannot change later; the code's minimum age "cannot contravene the Personal Status Law", but the code had not yet been written (four months to draft, then parliamentary approval); existing law: "18, or 15 with a judge's permission".
  **New text:** In 2025 Iraq amended its personal status law so that couples may choose to be governed by a Shiʿi (Jaʿfari) code instead of the 1959 law. Human rights groups fear it will allow lower marriage ages, but when Human Rights Watch reported in March 2025 the code had not yet been written; the 1959 law sets 18, or 15 with a judge's permission. (Later developments not checked.)

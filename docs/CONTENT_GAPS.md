# Content gaps and open questions

Things the site needs that the research docs do not (yet) provide, and places where a human check is needed. Nothing listed here is filled in by invention.

## Needs a reviewer
- **Transliteration.** Verse transliterations come from Quran.com's word-by-word data. They are consistent and sourced, but follow that dataset's own conventions (e.g. `l-laha`, `yukh'riju`, unmarked shadda) rather than a strict academic scheme. A reviewer should decide whether this is acceptable or whether a different source is needed.
- **Turkish wording.** All Turkish prose (except verse translations) is my translation of the English research. A Turkish-speaking theologian should read it for terminology (e.g. evidence labels: *Yerleşik bilgi* / *Yaygın yorum* / *Tartışmalı*).

## Missing from the research
- **S1 source titles.** The research links the 2020 *Science Advances* follow-up and the National Geographic article without their titles; the site describes them instead of quoting titles.

# Reflection · Tefekkür

*Signs for those who reflect.* (working title)

A bilingual (Turkish / English) website that invites people to look again: at the signs the Quran points to in nature, at its moral vision for life, and at the hard questions people carry about faith — answered honestly, with the limits of every answer shown.

> *a-fa-lā tatafakkarūn* — "Will you not reflect?"

Research and design stage. The site has not been built yet.

## Principles
- **Signs, not "scientific miracles."** The Quran is not a science textbook. Verses are shown as invitations to reflect; science is shown accurately and separately.
- **Honest notes everywhere.** Where a popular claim overstates, the site says so.
- **Doubts are respected.** Every hard question is shown in its strongest form before it is answered.
